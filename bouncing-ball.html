📖 HOW TO PLAY NAIL BLAST

🏓
Bouncing & Flapping:

Press SPACE, W, or tap the screen to give your ball an upward bounce. Gravity constantly pulls it downward.

📌
Sharp Nails & Spikes:

Nails sprout from the side walls, ceiling, and floor. Touching ANY nail will instantly BLAST your ball into pieces and end the game!

⭐
Scoring & Wall Bounces:

Every time you safely touch the left or right wall, you score +1 point, spikes switch positions, and new gems appear!

👥
2-Player Duel Mode:

Two balls enter the spiked pit! Player 1 uses W/SPACE, Player 2 uses ▲/ENTER. Survive longer than your opponent!
